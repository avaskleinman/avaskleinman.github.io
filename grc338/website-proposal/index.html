Home

Welcome to Brown Butter Bakery, a small locally owned bakery specializing in freshly baked cakes and delicous handcrafted cookies that taste just as amazing as they look! Grown from a passion for baking that was established at a young age, Brown Butter Bakery aims to bring sweet creations that are made with heart, creativity, and a love for celebrating life's special moments.

[A party spread of cakes and cookies]

At Brown Butter Bakery we cherish being a part of your special moments. Quality is at the center of everything we do, using fresh and carefully selected ingredients. Every fine detail is important to us, whether it be consulting on intial designs or the final decorative touch. Our confections are made to be show-stopping additions to your celbrations, big or small. Knowing our creations helped make your moment memorable is what inspires us creatively.

Thank you for supporting our small business and allowing us to be a part of your sweetest memories!

About

Our Story: Hello welcome to Brown Butter Bakery. At Brown Butter Bakery we sell more than just baked products, turning your sweetest ideas into edible works of art that are delicous and memorable. Our small bakery has been enhancing celbrations throughout Los Angeles, California since 2022.

What We Do: We turn milestones and everyday occasions into memorable edible moments. We believe custom desserts should feel just as special as the occasion they are made for. We work closely with each customer creating a genuine connection and listening to your ideas so we can bring your vision to life!

How We Do It: The best desserts are the ones that bring back memories, our flavors are inspired by comforting and nostalgic favorites. We bake with intention so that every bite will feel meaningful and memorable. Details matter just as much as flavor, we carefully craft each element to make sure your dessert is exactly what you envisioned.

Our First Location: In 2024 we opened our first physical location in Los Angeles, California. Being a part of the food community in Los Angeles means being surrounded by creativity, diversity, and unique culinary perspectives, we bring that same energy to everything we bake. Whether you are looking for a centerpiece cake, themed cookies, or something extra to add to your celebration, we are here to fufill that need and make your special moments a little sweeter.
