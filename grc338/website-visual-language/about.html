Hello welcome to Brown Butter Bakery. At Brown Butter Bakery we sell more than just baked products, turning your sweetest ideas into edible works of art that are delicous and memorable. Our small bakery has been enhancing celbrations throughout Los Angeles, California since 2022.

We turn milestones and everyday occasions into memorable edible moments. We believe custom desserts should feel just as special as the occasion they are made for. We work closely with each customer creating a genuine connection and listening to your ideas so we can bring your vision to life!

The best desserts are the ones that bring back memories, our flavors are inspired by comforting and nostalgic favorites. We bake with intention so that every bite will feel meaningful and memorable. Details matter just as much as flavor, we carefully craft each element to make sure your dessert is exactly what you envisioned.

In 2024 we opened our first physical location in Los Angeles, California. Being a part of the food community in Los Angeles means being surrounded by creativity, diversity, and unique culinary perspectives, we bring that same energy to everything we bake. Whether you are looking for a centerpiece cake, themed cookies, or something extra to add to your celebration, we are here to fufill that need and make your special moments a little sweeter.
